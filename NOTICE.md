# Source, attribution, and reuse

This is a curated research portfolio snapshot. Original-source hashes are recorded alongside the included implementation. The pattern engine uses pysubgroup, NumPy, pandas, and SciPy; these are installed as dependencies under their respective licenses.

The `twin/` subtree retains the existing twin project's Apache 2.0 license at `twin/LICENSE`. Its virtual-patient parameters are loaded from the user's installation of simglucose. This snapshot does not bundle the parameter table, T1DSim_AI code or weights, local-language-model weights, or research datasets.

The remaining original source, documentation, and app images are provided here for review. A broader redistribution license for those materials has not yet been selected. Dependency licenses continue to apply to their respective packages.

The app's bear illustration is a project asset developed with generative image tools and subsequent refinement. Screenshots were captured from the actual synthetic-only simulator route. Synthetic curves and example observations are demonstrations of the implementation.
